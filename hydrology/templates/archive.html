三类材料格式 
发布记录  {"source":"站A档案室","kind":"publish","no":"F-2024-001",
          "content":{"range_no":"R-7","rules_no":"G-7"}}
量程      {"source":"站A档案室","kind":"range","no":"R-7",
          "content":{"wl_min":0,"wl_max":120,"fl_min":0,"fl_max":1000}}
分区表    {"source":"站A档案室","kind":"rules","no":"G-7",
          "content":{"rules":[{"zone":"低水低流量区","priority":10,
                              "wl_min":0,"wl_max":49,"fl_min":0,"fl_max":399}, …]}}

也接受 {"kind":"rules","content":[…]}（内容直接为规则数组）， 以及 {"kind":"range","range":{…}} 等嵌套写法。类别也可写中文 “发布记录 / 量程 / 分区表”。
